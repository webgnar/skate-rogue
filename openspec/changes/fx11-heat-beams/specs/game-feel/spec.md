# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Searchlight and scan beam
From Heat 45 the cruiser SHALL throw a searchlight that hunts round the skater and locks on by Heat 85, and the
drone SHALL sweep a scan beam over the street while it is down, without flashing.

#### Scenario: Hunted at night
- **WHEN** Heat is 60 on a night floor
- **THEN** a white beam from the cruiser's roof wanders near the skater, its pool on the ground beside them
