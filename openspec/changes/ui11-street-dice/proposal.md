# ui11-street-dice

## Why
zack (2026-11-03): the end-of-run slot machine is meant to give the player something even when they lose, but "it
reads very out of place. a slot machine? its not a cool look. maybe rolling DICE would be cooler because thats
street." He wants it to feel like pinball's Match: rare, but super rewarding when it hits. He picked "dice now,
pinball later" on the decision card.

## What changes
- The slot machine is gone. Every run ends with one free roll of two dice on a curb under a street lamp; each chip
  (the old spin token, a red casino chip) found on the run adds a roll, 6 at most.
- A plain roll pays the pips halved (2 to 6 Stoken). Doubles pay a head start for the next run (2-2 and 3-3 $40,
  4-4 a free boon, 5-5 a saved continue), snake eyes a grip colour, and boxcars is the MATCH: the next run starts
  with 5 hearts instead of 3 (25 Stoken once 2 continues are already held).
- The first tap throws the dice, a tap mid-tumble lands them, the next goes to the summary. A gold MATCH! slams in.
- Pickup callouts say CHIP! +1 ROLL instead of SPIN TOKEN!.
- The GDD gets the dice section and the pinball last chance as a later idea (not built).
