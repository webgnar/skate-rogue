# Shop recomposed: bigger pictures, less text

## Why
zack (2026-10-28) sent a new shop mockup ("the shop UI screen should be composed more like this") and asked to
"emphasize the pictures. more visual please". The hand of cards hugged the floor under a big empty wall, the art
was small next to long effect text, and an upright phone squeezed nine cards into one row.

## What changes
- The hand fills the room under YOUR BOARD and YOUR GEAR: tall cards with the model art scaled up on its burst.
- Text is cut to two lines on a big screen and dropped on a phone (the hint strip says what to do); gear cards
  read "Yours: 85%".
- An upright phone stacks the header in two rows and the board over the gear, and deals the cards three to a row.
- Drag-to-buy, tap-to-buy and slot swaps are unchanged.
