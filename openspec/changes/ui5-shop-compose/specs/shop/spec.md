# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Picture-first shop cards
Shop cards SHALL fill the space under the setup with the picture taking most of each card, and on a phone SHALL
show no effect text.

#### Scenario: Upright phone
- **WHEN** the shop opens with nine cards on a 390x844 screen
- **THEN** the cards are dealt three to a row and every card is on screen
