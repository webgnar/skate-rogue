# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Smashing props
A prop the skater breaks SHALL burst into pieces with chips and dust in its material's colours, and a big smash
SHALL add a sound word and a camera kick scaled by speed, within the shared particle pool and a cap on live pieces.

#### Scenario: Landing on a crate
- **WHEN** the skater lands on a crate
- **THEN** the crate disappears, wood pieces tumble out with chips and dust, and KRAK! or CRUNCH! pops up

#### Scenario: Rolling through a bottle at cruising speed
- **WHEN** the skater rolls through a bottle at cruising speed
- **THEN** glass shards and sparkle burst out, with no word and no camera kick
