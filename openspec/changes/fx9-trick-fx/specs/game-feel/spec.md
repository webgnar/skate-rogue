# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Trick effects
Flip tricks SHALL leave a short trail in the trick's colour, grind sparks SHALL differ between metal rails and
concrete ledges, and a landing after a trick SHALL show as clean or sketchy, all within the one particle pool.

#### Scenario: Late catch
- **WHEN** the skater lands within 0.3 s of throwing a kickflip
- **THEN** a lopsided scuff of dust kicks out behind and no sparkle ring shows

#### Scenario: Clean kickflip
- **WHEN** the skater lands a kickflip thrown well before the landing
- **THEN** a cyan trail hangs where the board spun and a white sparkle ring flashes round the wheels
