# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Shop look from the mockup
Every shop card SHALL show a picture of what it is or does, coloured by its type, with its price on a tag, and the
shop SHALL fit an iPhone SE in landscape with five board slots.

#### Scenario: A gear card
- **WHEN** the wheels are worn and the shop opens
- **THEN** an orange NEW WHEELS card shows the game's wheel and its price

#### Scenario: A full board on a small phone
- **WHEN** the board has five slots on a 667x375 screen
- **THEN** all five slots and all four gear tiles are on screen
