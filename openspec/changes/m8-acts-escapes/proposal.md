# Proposal

## Why

The GDD's run is an escape across five acts with a scripted chase at the end of each, and beating act 5 wins.
Until now a run never ended except by bailing. zack asked (2026-10-24) to keep building out the GDD overnight.

## What Changes

- Acts: 5 acts of 3 floors. The HUD shows the act and floor.
- After each act's third floor, an escape floor: a street run with rush-hour traffic and Heat at 30, then the act's
  set piece, then the act gate. Act clear pays cash, Stoken and score; the road picked at the fork comes next.
- Roadblock set piece: a car, a bus, a line of cruisers and a second bus to car-hop across.
- Act 5's escape (two roadblocks) wins the run: a `YOU ESCAPED!` screen, and wins are saved.
- Reference bot learns to flip up onto a taller roof ahead, and every escape is validated with it.

Helicopter, drone swarm and the Hive core set pieces follow in their own changes.
