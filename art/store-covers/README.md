# Store covers

Skate Escape's cover art for store pages (CrazyGames first, docs/CRAZYGAMES.md).

- `capsule-original.png` (1672x941): zack's capsule art, the source.
- `cover-landscape-1920x1080.png`: the capsule scaled to CrazyGames' 16:9 cover.
- `cover-portrait-800x1200.png` and `cover-square-800x800.png`: zack's 2:3 and 1:1 versions.

CrazyGames' rules: no borders, no logos or icons, no text but the game's name.
