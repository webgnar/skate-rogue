Skate Escape

How to play

Tap anywhere to ollie. Tap again in the air to flip, and land on a rail or ledge to grind. Keep moving, keep your stoke up, and stay ahead of the AI. The pause button (top right) has the sound, music and effect settings.

Something wrong?

If the game crashes, a level can't be cleared, or the sound stops, tell us what happened and on which act (shown top left, like ACT 2-1), and which iPhone and iOS version you have.

Contact: zgeogoulet@gmail.com

Your save

Progress is saved on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes it, and there is no way for us to bring it back.

Privacy
