Privacy Policy

Skate Escape · last updated November 10, 2026

The short version

The Skate Escape app for iPhone collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you or how you play to us or anyone else.

The iPhone app

Your progress (unlocks, Stoken, your best scores) and your settings (sound, music, effects) are saved on your phone, inside the app. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you turn on tilt steering in the settings, the game reads your phone's motion sensor to lean the camera. That reading is used on the spot and is never stored or sent anywhere.

The app does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and does not ask for them.

The web version

The version played in a browser at skate-rogue.vercel.app keeps the same save in your browser's storage. It also has an online leaderboard: if you choose to post a score, we store the player tag you type, the score, the floor you reached and the date, and show them publicly on the board. Don't use your real name as a tag if you don't want it shown. The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for running and protecting the service.

The leaderboard is not in the iPhone app.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. The iPhone app collects none at all.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting anything (for example if in-app purchases or online features are added to the app), this page will say so before that version is released, and the date above will change.
